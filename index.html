How it works

Three steps from “thinking about it” to a listing you can share.

1

Tell us about your home

ZIP, size, bedrooms, what you paid, and when you bought it. That takes about five minutes.

2

Get a price estimate

We adjust what you paid by how values in your ZIP have moved since then, and compare it with similar homes nearby.

3

Download your listing sheet

A short Q&A fills in the details, then we draft MLS-ready fields, a description, and a print-ready PDF.

What this is: a pricing estimate and a listing draft to help you sell on your own.

What this isn't: we don't handle offers, contracts, payments, or closing. For those, talk to a real estate attorney.
